A local, receive-only spectrum inventory and I/Q investigation application for a single RTL-SDR receiver. Explore detections, revisit signals and inspect validated protocol metadata from one interface.

Experimental prerelease. The current Windows receiver can open but fails sample delivery. Recovery and live endurance are unverified. Classification scores do not establish a protocol.

Discover

Bounded scans, resumable coverage and private signal history. Invalid or unvisited intervals stay visible.

Investigate

DSP measurements, analog monitoring, a Signal Lab and selected digital bit candidates with explicit limits.

Verify

Selected decoders require framing and integrity checks. Optional AI hypotheses stay separate from verified evidence.

Your receiver. Your data.

Core operation needs no account or cloud API. Acquisition starts idle. Frame and identifier retention default off. RF Atlas does not transmit, decrypt protected traffic, replace USB drivers or identify physical transmitters from a shared frequency.
